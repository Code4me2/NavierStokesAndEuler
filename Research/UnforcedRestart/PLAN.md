## Fixed mathematical conventions

* `Space := NavierStokes.ProblemStatement.Space = EuclideanSpace ℝ (Fin 3)`; `SpaceTime = ℝ × Space`. Internal fields take `(t,x)`. Comparator fields take `x` then `t`; use checked `ComparatorBridge.toComparator/fromComparator`, not an implicit swap.
* Whole space is R³ with Lebesgue volume. Periodic means unit coordinate periods on R³ (the unit torus); use the existing `UnitSpatialPeriodsOn` and cube integral, not whole-space integrals of periodic fields. Velocity **and pressure** must be periodic for periodic comparison. No extra periodic energy field is present in the delivered Comparator class.
* Default viscosity is **one** because `ProblemStatement.navierStokesResidual` is `∂t u + Du(u) - Δu + ∇p`. Write `Rν` only with an explicit new local definition or the Comparator PDE, always with `ν>0`; no silent viscosity parameter in a viscosity-one result.
* Original singular time is `1`; fix `0 < t₀ < 1` for an interior restart. New time is `s=t-t₀`; horizon `H=1-t₀>0`; `uᵣ(s,x)=u(t₀+s,x)`, likewise pressure/force, and datum `a(x)=u(t₀,x)`. Slabs are `[0,S]×R³`, `0<S<H`; original comparison slabs are `[t₀,t₀+S]`. Endpoint `s=H` is never presumed smooth. Separate whole future `[0,∞)` from a presingular half-open interval.
* Smooth on a closed slab means `ContDiffOn ℝ ∞` on that slab. PDE differentiation occurs on the **open** time interior unless a within-derivative boundary theorem is proved. Comparator uses `derivWithin` at time zero; internal temporal derivative is a full derivative. Translation at an interior old time helps, but does not automatically discharge every boundary convention.
* **Critical signature trap:** `ProblemStatement.Solution` hardcodes `u(0,x)=0`, even on a restricted time set. `Solution.mono` is restriction, NOT restart with arbitrary data. Do not use `GlobalSolutionOne`, `MaximalLifespan.ClassicalSolution`, or a zero-datum adapter as a general restart structure. State explicit field hypotheses or introduce a task-local nonzero-datum structure.
* Difference convention: forced reference `(u,p,f)`, unforced comparator `(v,q,0)`, `w=u-v`, `r=p-q`. At viscosity one the intended equation is `∂t w = Δw - Du(w) - Dw(v) - ∇r + f`. Swapping reference changes the decomposition; state it.
* Let `E=∫|w|²` (no half) and `D=∫|∇w|²`, with the domain/gradient convention defined explicitly. Then formally `E'/2 + νD = -∫⟨w,Du(w)⟩ + ∫⟨w,f⟩`. Any different normalization must be declared. A scalar differential inequality is not a proof that these actual PDE integrals obey it.
* Whole-space compact support belongs to the constructed reference, NOT the competitor. Preserve velocity/pressure smoothness, divergence freedom, integrability, slab-uniform competitor energy, and actual pressure-flux hypotheses. Do not assume pressure decay implicitly. Smooth compact support at a fixed positive time supplies admissible restart decay only after a proof for all derivatives and real decay exponents.
* Force absorption sign: `Rν(u,p)=∇φ` gives `Rν(u,p-φ)=0`. Periodic φ must be periodic (or an explicitly verified equivalent admissible pressure correction); a constant vector force need not be a periodic gradient. Time-only pressure gauges change no gradient.
